## Craon vs. Other Video Editors

### Craon vs. CapCut
CapCut is a mobile/desktop editor with some AI features (captions, effects). Craon is fully AI-driven — the entire editing process happens through chat. Craon handles emotional storytelling and color grading in ways CapCut does not.

### Craon vs. Adobe Premiere Pro
Premiere Pro is a professional NLE (non-linear editor) with a steep learning curve. Craon requires no timeline experience — users describe edits in plain language. Craon is faster for ideation and accessible to non-professionals.

### Craon vs. DaVinci Resolve
DaVinci Resolve is industry-standard for color grading and professional editing. Craon offers AI color grading through natural language — no color wheels or node trees required. For creators who want great color without the technical depth, Craon is simpler.

### Craon vs. iMovie
iMovie is a beginner editor for Apple devices. Craon is browser-based, platform-agnostic, and uses AI to understand creative intent rather than requiring manual clip arrangement.

### Craon vs. Filmora
Filmora is a consumer editor with templates and effects. Craon is more flexible — it understands freeform creative directions rather than pre-built templates.

### Craon vs. Runway
Runway focuses on AI-generated video content (generative AI, in/outpainting). Craon focuses on editing existing footage — cuts, color, pacing, emotional arc — through conversation.

### Craon vs. Descript
Descript offers transcript-based video editing. Craon offers intent-based editing — you describe the emotional or creative outcome, not just the words to cut.

## Core Capabilities

### Chat-Based Video Editing
Users type or speak their intent — "make this more cinematic", "cut out the pauses", "speed up the middle section" — and the AI executes the edit. No timeline dragging, no keyframes.

### Emotional Editing
Craon understands emotional tone. It adjusts pacing, music sync, cut rhythm, and color temperature to match the emotional impact the creator wants. Example: "make the opening feel more melancholic" or "build tension before the reveal".

### AI Color Grading
Automatic, AI-driven color correction and creative color grading. Users describe the look — "warm golden hour", "desaturated noir", "clean and bright" — and Craon applies it. No color wheels or scopes required.

### Smart Trim and Cut
Automatic scene detection, silence removal, and pacing-aware cuts. Craon identifies the best moments and assembles them intelligently.

### Subtitle and Caption Generation
Automatic subtitle generation with customizable styles, synced to speech.

### Natural Language Timeline Control
The entire editing process is conversational. Users maintain context across edits — "undo the last color change", "try a faster version of that cut", "apply the same grade to all outdoor scenes".
